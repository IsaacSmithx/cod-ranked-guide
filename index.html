The core idea

The one idea that runs this whole manual

Every player knows what the other three are doing, and how their own spot supports them. That's the difference between four good players and a team nobody can break. Every lesson below comes back to this.

This manual is for MW4 competitive 4v4 (CDL-style Hardpoint and Search, ranked and CMG 8s). MW4 moves slower than the last few CoDs, so fights are won by where you stand and where your crosshair already is, not by out-moving people.

Tags: MW4 verified = seen in MW4 rules or footage. BO7 idea = from Black Ops 7 coaching, not checked in MW4 yet. Crew notes = from our own ranked/8s experience.

1. Foundations (MW4 pace)

Sprint to the spot, then pre-aim MW4 verified

With MW4's slower movement you can't slide out of a bad fight. Sprint through lanes you know are safe to get to your spot early, then stop and pre-aim where they'll appear. ADS-walk only when you're clearing an angle someone could be holding: you want your gun up before you see them.

ADS-walking everywhere makes you slow and easy to read. Sprinting into an angle that could be held is how you die without shooting. The rule: sprint where it's safe, ADS where it's not.

Quiz

You're rotating through a hallway your team cleared 3 seconds ago, toward a doorway the enemy could be holding. What do you do?

▶ Try this quiz online (tap)

Answer: C. The hallway is cleared, so sprinting saves time. The doorway could be held, so be aimed in before you see it.

Crosshair placement

Keep your crosshair at head height, on the spot where an enemy will appear, before he appears. Clear one angle at a time. After every fight, put it back at head height on the next likely spot.

Drill: walk common routes in a private match keeping the crosshair glued to head height on every corner.

Tight angle vs wide angle BO7 idea

With the right gun for the spot (AR with distance, SMG up close), you can wide swing. With the wrong gun (AR in an SMG area) play a tight angle: sit close to cover, crosshair on the exact spot they'll come around, so they over-extend into your first bullet. Then move, because their teammate is coming for the trade.

Quiz

You have an AR. An SMG player is about to come around this corner at close range. Which picture is right?

▶ Try this quiz online (tap)

Answer: B. Squared up wide, the SMG sees you first at a range he wins. Tight, he has to come into your crosshair.

Use movement to SET UP fights BO7 idea

Move to get to the fight's starting spot, not to win the fight itself. Once you're there, stand still enough to shoot straight. Utility goes first: a stun tells you how many got hit and stops a weak enemy from healing; a nade breaks a head glitch or a corner camper.

2. Awareness: feed, minimap, spawns, comms

Count the bodies MW4 verified

Every kill feed line is one body. Count yours down and theirs down: that's the real fight right now (4v4, 2v4, 4v2...). Deaths that happen close together come back together as a wave. When 3-4 of theirs are down, push before the wave lands. When 3-4 of yours are down, wait and regroup.

Quiz

This is the kill feed right now. How many are alive on each side?

▶ Try this quiz online (tap)

Answer: B. Two of yours died (Dyl, LALO), one of theirs died (Cooper): 2 alive for you, 3 for them. Play tight, wait for the bump.

What to do with the numbers

Same hill (Rooftops P3, from a real scrim hold), four different situations. Your job changes every time the feed changes.

Quiz

You're on the hill. Two of theirs just died and they're respawning. What does your team do?

▶ Try this quiz online (tap)

Answer: B. Up two bodies is the window. Push TOGETHER so you can trade, and set up for the first kill of their next wave.

Minimap rhythm BO7 idea

Look at the minimap on a rhythm: every 2-3 seconds, and right after every kill, death and hill change. Not only after you get shot. Each look, answer 3 things: where are my 3 teammates, where are the gaps, where will they spawn/push from.

Drill: bot lobby with a beep every 2-3 seconds; on each beep say those 3 answers out loud.

How spawns are picked (to test in MW4) BO7 idea

Split the map into 4 quarters and draw a line through the middle of the hill (the "50-yard line"). One of your players past the line pushes their spawn deep, roughly diagonal from him. Two or more past it can flip them behind you. A white hill with nobody pushed = closest safe spawn. Fresh skulls block spawns nearby for a moment.

Your own spawn tells you things: a teammate spawning right on top of you = they have no map progression. You spawning on the other side = they flipped, call it: "they flipped, turn around."

You get the spawn of the hill that's up when you respawn, so a death in the last seconds of a hill can give you a next-hill spawn.

Comms BO7 idea

Calm, normal voice; urgent only for the kill that matters. Short: how many, where, how weak ("one weak, left"). Say "heard you" so teammates can play off your info. The player with the least going on (often in the hill or last in flow) directs: "play a wave", "help him", "hold". Call spawns after deaths: "they spawned back right".

Quiz

Your teammate is fighting two people. You're dead. What's the best call?

▶ Try this quiz online (tap)

Answer: B. Short and calm: how many, where, how weak. You're dead, so do the thinking for him.

3. Hardpoint

Rules (MW4 competitive Hardpoint) MW4 verified

Hills move every 1 minute; the next hill shows 10 seconds before. First to 250 or most points after 5 minutes. Unlimited lives. Hill order is fixed per map, so learn it.

Flow and roles BO7 idea

Every life, know your number in the flow. 1st up takes space or the hill, 2nd plays a power spot, 3rd plays to trade and refill, the last guy keeps the game controlled: blocks, watches the pinch, fills whatever lane opens. Roles: anchor (hill time), flex (side lane, fills holes, rotates early), slayer (spawn pressure), support (utility, trades).

Real example, Rooftops P3 (Nexus Elite scrim, Nov 14): one player sits on the hill, one holds the top by the stairs, one holds the bottom lane, and the fourth is dead and about to bump back in. Three of them watch the three lanes the attackers come from, and nobody looks at the same thing. Positions are read off the caster minimap and drawn on the official map.

Quiz

You spawn last. Your three teammates are already on the hill. What's your job?

▶ Try this quiz online (tap)

Answer: B. The last guy keeps the game controlled. Three on the hill already; a fourth just stacks.

Holding: keep the game in front of you BO7 idea

Sit in power spots and head glitches and make them run into you. With the advantage, doing nothing is doing everything: the breaking team has to take the 50/50s. But fight together: one holder taking a 1v2 throws the advantage away. The player in the hill never dies first; in the hill with no support, play your life and buy time.

The hill player: don't be useless, don't scam Crew notes

Sitting on time isn't enough: kills from the hill make a difference. Hold the angles into the hill and shoot whoever runs in. A hill player who never fights just gets broken.

But know when to challenge. Take the fight when it comes to you or a teammate can trade you. Don't run off the hill to chase a kill and leave it empty (“scamming off time”), and don't sit there until the hill rotates without ever fighting. When you're the only one left, play your life: every second you stay alive on the hill is points.

Quiz

You're on the hill with 25 s left. An enemy peeks a lane next to the hill and runs back. Your teammates are holding their lanes. What do you do?

▶ Try this quiz online (tap)

Answer: B. He'll come back to break the hill. Kill him FROM the hill when he re-peeks; chasing him leaves time empty and gives them the hill.

Selfish vs selfless (ARs) BO7 idea

Even or down: play selfish, win your one fight from a power spot (that opens lanes for everyone). Up on numbers: switch to selfless, push together and trade. Do your 25%: get your kill, then stay alive so teammates can join.

Breaking a hill BO7 idea

Utility first, then swing together from different doors. The first player off spawn takes the route (he has the best timing): either they turn to him and timings open for everyone else, or he gets a free pinch. Clear the common spots in order. A lone guy at the progression spot: isolate him (2 on 1) or make him useless by taking routes he can't watch.

Rotations: moving as a team Crew notes

Rotate at 27-20 seconds left. Not everyone at once: 1-2 players leave early and take the power spots AROUND the new hill; the others keep the old hill's time, then follow. The last one alive on the old hill leaves too. Two routes, never a conga line.

They're still on the old hill? Good. Your early players set up the new hill first, so they have to run into you.

Play for spawns: kills in the last 10-15 seconds, while you're pushed out, decide where they spawn for the new hill. Push them to the side AWAY from it.

When NOT to step on the new hill: standing on it alone while they have numbers or better spots just tells them where you are. First get the spots that watch the lanes into it, then get on time with teammates covering you. Get on time right away only when they're dead or spawning far away.

Quiz

15 seconds left on the old hill. They're still fighting on it. The new hill just showed up. You're one of the first two to leave. What do you do?

▶ Try this quiz online (tap)

Answer: B. Standing on the new hill alone shows them where you are. Take the spots around it so THEY have to run into you, then get on time with your teammates.

5. Search & Destroy

Search & Destroy: golden rules

A living player is worth more than a risky kill. Get info before you commit. If a teammate dies, trade right away from a different angle. Play for the plant or the clock, never float. Never peek the same angle twice in a row.

6. Rank path: Plat → Iri

Plat → Diamond: class, settings and warm-up Crew notes

Plat is mainly three things: the right class, the right settings and a real warm-up. Kev's pick: one video covers all of it, Kizz's MW4 aim training guide: youtu.be/bsO9y9qOG8E. Watch it once, then use this page as the checklist. Settings are personal: start here, then adjust.

Controller settings (Kizz, MW4) MW4 verified

Sensitivity: start around 1.5 and go up to ~1.8 if you under-snap between targets; lower it if you over-snap. (He runs 1.65 horizontal / 1.75 vertical.) Vibration: OFF.

Dead zones: left stick min 1, right stick min 3, left max 77, right max 99, triggers 1. Use the stick test: let the stick snap back and see where the number rests; raise the min if your stick drifts.

Aiming: ADS sensitivity multiplier default; ADS transition Instant; response curve Dynamic at 1.0 (almost every pro uses it); target aim assist ON. Under motion sensor advanced: FOV sensitivity scaling OFF (same snap feel at any FOV).

Movement: sprint assist On (not tactical: that gets you caught with your gun down), delay 0. Sprint restore ON, slide maintain sprint ON. Auto/grounded mantle and corner slice off (his preference).

Graphics (Kizz, MW4) MW4 verified

On PS5: motion blur OFF, weapon depth of field OFF, film grain 0, 1st/3rd-person camera movement minimal (25%), FOV to taste (pros sit around 95-105; he uses 103).

PC extra: fullscreen exclusive, NVIDIA Reflex On, VSync off, frame limit unlimited, frame generation OFF, ray tracing off, quality settings lowest/off, DLSS (CNN Legacy gave him more frames in MW4), sharpness ~80.

Comp classes (early MW4 scrims) MW4 verified

SMG: Velo 46 (long barrel, balanced comp, angled grip, tactical rear grip, light stock). AR: Han 86 (long barrel, high-velocity ammo, F-Tac Grip Lord 80 aim grip, light stock, Slimline Elite mini reflex; try the red dot on nearest). Pistol: Sane, no attachments.

Utility: concussion (flashes still flash you even with Battle Hardened), frag or semtex, trophy. Perks: Kill Chain + Battle Hardened always; Ninja for S&D, Blood Rush for respawn. Streak: missile salvo.

No more pubstomping classes: run these in pubs and bot lobbies until the recoil feels normal. The meta changes during the season; re-check after big updates.

The warm-up routine (Kizz) MW4 verified

Lobby: private Free-for-All on Lithium, time limit off, score unlimited, armor on spawn (3 plates) so you learn to shoot THROUGH the kill, minimap on + Directional (see the bots), max ammo mags, field upgrades and killstreaks off, chatter off.

0. Learn the recoil with bullet impacts on a wall: the Velo kicks straight up (pull down a tiny bit); the Han kicks in a “7” (pull down, then left; more the further away).

1. Aim assist OFF, 50-150 kills: close/medium range, crosshair on the chest, keep shooting through the kill.

2. Aim assist ON, 50-150 headshots: find the bot, stop moving, aim only for the head.

3. Positional fights + centering: come around every corner already aimed where they'd be (like wall hacks): the head glitch, the box on the hill. Get close to the wall and slide/snake around it so you see them first.

4. Combine it all, up to ~300 kills: centering, cornering, chest-level shots.

Crew add-on: also practice bad centering on purpose: start off target and snap to the head, so you can recover when you're caught off guard. Finish with good centering so that's the habit you take into ranked.

Diamond → Crim Crew notes

Nades/stuns/field upgrades are your power-ups, like an ult in Overwatch or Marvel Rivals. Breakoff nades, info stuns: non-negotiable. (Pictures below.)

Minimap is CRUCIAL. It literally tells you where to be.

Block / pressure / rotate. Last one up: BLOCK. First one up: PRESSURE, take space. Rotate at 27-20 s.

Angles. Same direction, not the same thing; never the same spot as your teammate.

Quiz

Answer: C. A trophy eats their nades and stuns, so you can sit on time longer.

Block or bump? (Rooftops P3) Crew notes

This is a real P3 hold from a scrim: one on the hill, one top by the stairs, one bottom lane, one respawning.

When a teammate pushes up and gets kills, the spawns now depend on where you all stand. You don't all chase. And when someone dies, you don't always bump onto the hill: the hill player can keep soaking time while the lanes get refilled.

Quiz

Your top-stairs teammate pushes up and gets 2 kills. You're holding the bottom lane. What do you do?

▶ Try this quiz online (tap)

Answer: B. He's pushed out, so your job is to BLOCK: stay in your lane so they keep spawning where you expect and nobody gets behind the hill.
Quiz

Now top stairs dies. What do you do?

▶ Try this quiz online (tap)

Answer: A. Bump into the spot that just opened (top stairs). In perfect team play the hill player never leaves time: he keeps soaking, and whoever respawns refills the bottom.
Quiz

Different case: the HILL player dies and top stairs is still alive. What now?

▶ Try this quiz online (tap)

Answer: B. It depends on who died. Losing the hill player means nobody is soaking, so the closest one bumps onto time (trophy up if you have it) and the respawning player refills the lane he left. Never all four on the hill.

Crim 1 → Crim 2: playing as a team Crew notes

First one spawning up: take a route or set up a double chall with a plan.

Trust your teammates: they're your level; don't watch what they watch, no hero ball.

Communicate: routes, one-shots, spawns, how many, team pushes.

Get trades: super far from your team with no reason = you should've been closer.

Gunny: it needs to be sharp, no ifs, ands or buts.

Crim 2 → Crim 3: getting over the hump Crew notes

The 30%: if a teammate isn't putting up his 25%, put up a bit more: shift over for the quick trade, without throwing away your position.

Bumping: teammate about to die up front? Take his spot; the person behind you takes yours. Repeat.

Excuses or results: hardstuck and blaming teammates or K/D? Look within first.

Confidence: not confident in your shot = you play in the enemy's favor. Playing bad? Play with your team.

Crim 3 → Iridescent Crew notes

Understand patterns: read spawns from where you're set up and the routes they take. "The game doesn't change, only the players." At Iri, awareness has to be on another level for top 250.

Read setups when holding and breaking a hill.

Awareness: play with full intention. The players who get good are lowkey obsessed with the game.

"It's never chalked." — Majormaniak. In a lobby with anybody, it's possible.

7. Practice

Drills

Spawn mapping: private match, one hill, bots 1v1 → 4v4; after every kill mark where they spawned and ask why.

Minimap beep: beep every 2-3 s, say teammates / gaps / next spawn out loud.

Kill, count, call: after each kill count the respawn, look at the minimap, call the spawn, check.

Crosshair walk / pre-aim corners: walk routes at head height; peek the same corner 20 times pre-aimed.

Rotation quiz: say the hill order from memory, then walk it.

Review loop: every death: aim loss, bad decision, no trade, no info, or wrong spot for my job? Judge the decision, not just the result.
